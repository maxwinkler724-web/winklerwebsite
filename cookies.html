Cookie Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

The short answer: this site sets no cookies, and it loads nothing from advertisers, analytics companies or social networks. That is why you do not see a cookie banner.

What the site stores in your browser

A few pages save your own settings or progress in your browser's local storage, which works like a note kept on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is used only for the thing you asked for.

NameWhereWhat it remembers
fixitvan.v1, fixitvan.snd, fixitvan.langFix-It Van gameStars earned, sound on or off, language
hvacsim.*, sim.langService Call SimulatorWhich calls you finished, language

The kids' game also keeps an offline copy of its own files in your browser (a "service worker" cache) so it works without internet. It holds only the game's files.

To remove all of this, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Nothing else is affected.

Other companies' cookies

  • Stripe: when checkout opens and you press a Buy button, you leave this site for a Stripe payment page. Stripe may set cookies there for security and fraud prevention under its cookie policy.
  • Links: lessons link to sources on other sites. Those sites have their own cookie rules once you visit them.

If this changes

Advertising, analytics, accounts and affiliate links are switched off. If we ever turn one on that uses cookies or similar tracking, we will update this page first and, where the law requires it, ask your permission before anything non-essential is set. The kids' games will stay free of ads, analytics and cookies.
