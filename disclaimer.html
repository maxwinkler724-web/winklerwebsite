Safety first. Mechanical, electrical, gas, refrigerant, steam and boiler work can cause serious injury, death, fire, explosion and property damage. Follow manufacturer instructions, lockout/tagout, your employer's safety program and local code. Wear the right protective equipment. Numbers shown are typical examples for learning, not setpoints or design values: use the nameplate, drawings and manufacturer data for your equipment.

Emergencies. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds, get everyone out into fresh air and call 911. Do not use a lesson to troubleshoot an emergency.

Licensed work. Only do work you are trained, licensed and legally permitted to do where you are. In New Jersey, for example, HVACR contracting, electrical and plumbing work and boiler operation each need their own license. Buying, handling or recovering most refrigerants requires EPA Section 608 certification.

Kids' games. The games teach ideas, not hands-on repair. Children should never open furnaces, water heaters, outdoor units or electrical panels. Real repair work is for trained, licensed adults.
