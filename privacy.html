What we collect

  • Nothing by default. In the default setup the site loads no ads, trackers or third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site.
  • If ads are turned on, the ad network may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads. You can accept or reject non-essential use in the banner. Your choice is saved in your browser.
  • If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.
  • Email signup (optional): if you type your address into a signup form, it goes to our email service provider (named here when chosen) and is used only to send new lessons and study tips. Every email has an unsubscribe link. The signup form is off until a provider is set up, and it never appears on the game pages.

Payments

When you press a Buy button you leave this site and pay on a checkout page run by Square. Square collects and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card number. We receive your name, email and order details from Square so we can send your files and answer questions. If you choose to pay by Zelle, the payment happens in your own bank app and we see the sender name and amount your bank shows us.

If a PayPal link is shown, paying there works the same way: PayPal handles your payment details under its own privacy policy and tells us your name, email and order.

Accounts

An account is optional. When accounts are switched on, you can sign in with Google or with an email and password. The sign-in service is Google Firebase Authentication. It stores your email address, a hashed password (if you use one), and the sign-in details Google shares with it. Nothing from Firebase loads on any page except the account page. We do not sell this data, and we use it only to run your account. To delete your account, write to us from the email address on the account.
