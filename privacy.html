Payments

When you press a Buy button you leave this site and pay on a checkout page run by Square. Square collects and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card number. You can pay by credit or debit card or a digital wallet such as Apple Pay or Google Pay. We receive your name, email and order details so we can send your files and answer questions.
