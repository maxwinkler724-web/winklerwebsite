Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Trade Study Bench ("we", "us") is an independent educational publisher run by Max Winkler, an individual based in New Jersey, United States. This policy explains what information this website and our study products collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: winklerhvac724@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not use analytics, ads, tracking pixels, social media widgets or embedded videos. The site loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies.
  • We set no cookies. A few pages save your own settings or game progress in your browser; that never leaves your device. See the Cookie Policy.
  • There are no forms that send us anything today. You can email us if you choose to.
  • We never sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • The kids' games collect nothing at all.

Payments

When checkout opens, pressing a Buy button takes you to a payment page run by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details under its own privacy policy. Stripe may use cookies on its own checkout page for security and fraud prevention; those are Stripe's, not ours. We receive your name, email and order details so we can deliver your files and help you.

Features that are not switched on

The site's code includes some features that are turned off: an email newsletter sign-up, user accounts (Google sign-in), advertising, affiliate links, and PayPal or Zelle payment. None of them run today, and none of them collect anything. Before we switch any of them on, we will update this policy and the Cookie Policy first, say exactly what is collected and by whom, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share information only with the service providers that do a job for us (GitHub to host the site, Stripe to take payments, Google Gmail for our email), or when the law requires it.

Children

The main site is study material for adults and teens learning a trade. It is not directed to children under 13. Anyone under 18 should buy products only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If we learn that we received personal information from a child under 13 (for example, by email) without a parent's consent, we will delete it. Parents can write to winklerhvac724@gmail.com.

The kids' games (Fix-It Van and the Service Call Simulator) collect no personal information: no accounts, ads, analytics, cookies or network requests. Stars and settings stay in the browser on your own device. See the kids' games privacy notice.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it: email winklerhvac724@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. We may keep order records the tax laws require.
  • Clear your saved progress and settings any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: we do not track you across sites, so the site works the same either way. If we ever add a feature that shares data for advertising, we will treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out.
  • Some laws (for example in New Jersey, California, the EU or the UK) give these rights formally. We offer them to everyone.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We keep as little data as we can, because data we do not hold cannot leak. No method is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will post any change here with a new effective date before it takes effect.
