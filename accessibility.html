Accessibility

Updated: October 16, 2026

We want every lesson, practice tool and game here to work for everyone, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or high-contrast settings. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2) at level AA.

What we do

  • Every page on the main site has a "Skip to content" link, a page language and one main heading, and works with the keyboard alone (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and Space).
  • Form fields have visible labels, and the focused item gets a clear outline.
  • Images have text descriptions. Product covers say what the product is.
  • On the main site, text and button colors are checked against the WCAG contrast ratio of 4.5 to 1, in light and dark mode.
  • The site follows your device's dark mode and lets you zoom.

Known limits

  • Some games and simulators use gauges and drawings that may not have a full text description yet.
  • Downloadable PDFs are not yet fully tagged for screen readers. If you need a different format, email us and we will send one.
  • We test with automated checks and by hand, not yet with a professional audit.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, email winklerhvac724@gmail.com with the page and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 business days and to fix it or give you the content another way.
