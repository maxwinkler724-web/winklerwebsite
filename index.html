Common questions

Is this free?

The lessons, troubleshooting trees, quizzes and games are free. The optional study packs on the Store page are one-time downloads.

Will this give me my EPA 608 certification?

No. EPA 608 certification comes from taking an exam given by an EPA-approved certifying organization. Our lessons and practice questions help you prepare. Start with the EPA 608 guide and check the EPA Section 608 page for current rules.

Can I fix my own furnace or AC after reading a lesson?

Some checks are safe for homeowners, like filters and thermostat batteries. Gas, refrigerant and high-voltage work needs a licensed professional. Each lesson says where that line is.

Where do the facts come from?

Every lesson lists its sources at the bottom: manufacturer manuals, government agencies and trade references. Questions are written in original wording.

Do you collect my data?

No ads, no trackers, and visitor data is never sold. See the privacy page.
