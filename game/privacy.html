For parents and teachers: privacy in our games

Fix-It Van and the Service Call Simulator are free and collect no personal information.

  • No accounts, names, emails, photos, voice or location.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no social or chat features.
  • The game makes no network requests that send data anywhere. A security setting in the page blocks them.
  • Stars, language and sound choices are saved only in the browser on your own device. Clearing site data erases them.
  • A name typed on the certificate is never stored or sent; it is only drawn on the screen for printing.
  • After the first visit the browser keeps the game's own files so it can open offline. Those files come only from this site.
  • Links on game pages that lead to the rest of the site are marked "For parents and teachers". The rest of the site is training material for adults and may show ads.
  • We never sell or share children's data, because we never collect it.
