Training simulator for students and adults. A fictional gas hot-water boiler with two zones. Boiler work is regulated: follow manufacturer instructions and local code. Real refrigerant work requires EPA 608 certification. Collects and sends nothing.

Select a part, then choose a test. System data: cold fill about 12 psi (illustrative; use the boiler maker's fill instructions), expansion tank precharge matched to fill pressure, relief valve 30 psi, aquastat 160 to 180 F. Cast-iron boilers: keep return water at or above about 135 F (Burnham manuals) to avoid flue-gas condensation and thermal shock.

    Sources and how numbers are labeled

    Where the facts in these calls come from. Links open other sites; the simulator itself makes no network calls. All sources

    • NJ Bureau of Boiler and Pressure Vessel Compliance advisory on testing boilers after maintenance or repair (Sept. 6, 2023). GOV. Basis for the post-service safety test and recording set points. The CSD-1 standard itself is paywalled and not taught here.
    • Burnham Commercial: CSD-1 requirements bulletin. MFR. Which controls and safety devices are required on hot-water and steam boilers.
    • Burnham V11H boiler manual. MFR. Return water temperature and cold-water limits used in the fill and thermal-shock content.
    • Caleffi Idronics 2: air and dirt elimination in hydronic systems. MFR. Expansion tank on the circulator inlet side; at least 5 psi at the top of the system.
    • Caleffi: closely spaced tees. MFR. Closely spaced tees give hydraulic separation; offsets and bends break it. No spacing figure is given.
    • Xylem / McDonnell & Miller control catalog. MFR. Low-water cutoff types and testing.
    • National Board: suggested maintenance log program. GUIDE.
    • Zurich risk guide: hot water heating boiler inspection and maintenance. TRADE/insurer.

    How numbers are labeled. Readings, prices, set points, timers, blink codes and trip values in the calls are practice examples made up for the game unless a call says a source gives them. Words like about, example and practice mark them. Where a number comes from one maker's document (for example Hoshizaki timers or Trane flash codes), it is that maker's example and other makers differ. Rules of thumb (such as coolers about 35 to 38 F) are common practice, not rules. Laws and codes are cited by name: confirm the current text. Always follow the equipment maker's instructions and local code.
