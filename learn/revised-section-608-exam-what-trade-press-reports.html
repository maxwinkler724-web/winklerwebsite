The Revised Section 608 Exam: What Trade Press Reports

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Part of the EPA 608 Guide

Rumors about a new 608 test travel fast. Here is what has actually been reported, who said it, and what we cannot confirm from an EPA page.

Where this comes from

A September 2026 article in ACHR News, quoting an official of the ESCO Institute from a webinar, describes a revision of the Section 608 question bank. We are reporting that article, not an EPA announcement. EPA's own certification pages, when we read them (the requirements page showed an update date of July 15, 2026), list approved testing programs and say nothing about a revised exam or a 2027 date. Treat everything below as trade press, and confirm with the organization that gives your test.

What the article reports

  • It describes the first major revision of the question bank in a very long time.
  • Exams written from the current bank can only be given through January 1, 2027, per the ESCO official and the ACHR News article (this is the magazine's own reporting, not an EPA statement). Both versions may run for the rest of 2026.
  • The new bank is described as roughly 400 questions, with test providers building about ten versions.
  • The four sections stay: Core, Type I, Type II and Type III. Universal still means passing all four.
  • There is no new Type IV for A2L refrigerants.
  • New general-knowledge questions on A2L refrigerants such as R-32, R-454B and R-1234yf, and on A3 hydrocarbons, are added. R-22 questions may now mention R-32 or R-454B too.
  • AIM Act requirements are described as not tested, since they come from separate law.

What does not change

The article says existing certification cards remain valid and there is no federal requirement to retest. EPA's page agrees in spirit: credentials issued by programs while they were approved are still valid. Someone with one certification type can finish Universal later, though sections taken after January 1, 2027 would come from the revised bank, per the article.

What it means for studying

Expect the exam to stay a regulatory and safety overview, which is how the ESCO official described it, rather than a hands-on skills test. Learn the basics of safety classes, why A2L refrigerants are mildly flammable, and the fact that nothing replaces manufacturer instructions. Our EPA 608 Basics and A2L and A3 Refrigerants lessons cover the concepts. This site does not publish exam items.

Still open

We could not find an EPA statement on the question-bank change, so the cutoff date and the details of the new content remain a trade-press report. Ask your testing organization for its current schedule and study outline. Note too that A2L work calls for manufacturer training and job-site rules beyond the exam.
