EPA 608 Guide

Last updated: 2026-10-16

EPA Section 608 is the federal certification for people who work on equipment that contains regulated refrigerants. The rules come from the Clean Air Act and are written out in 40 CFR Part 82, Subpart F. This guide collects every free 608 lesson on the site in a sensible study order, so you can see the whole subject instead of hunting through a long list.

Start with the basics so the vocabulary is familiar: what Core, Type I, Type II and Type III mean, what ozone depletion and the Montreal Protocol have to do with the rules, and the difference between recovering, recycling and reclaiming refrigerant. Then move to the hands-on topics that exam writers and inspectors both care about: recovery steps, cylinders and labels, evacuation, leak repair and the records a technician keeps.

When the reading feels comfortable, switch to practice. The flashcards help with vocabulary and numbers, and the practice quiz shows why each answer is right. All questions are written in our own words, with no real exam questions copied. Exam formats and rules change, so confirm the current details on the EPA and your testing organization pages before you register.
