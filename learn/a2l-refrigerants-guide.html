A2L Refrigerants Guide

Last updated: 2026-10-16

A2L refrigerants are the newer, lower global-warming-potential refrigerants that are replacing R-410A in many new residential and light commercial systems. The A2L class means "lower toxicity, mildly flammable." That one word, flammable, changes how equipment is designed, installed and serviced, even though real-world ignition is hard to achieve.

This guide groups the lessons in the order a technician meets the topic. First, what the A2L and A3 classes mean and how R-454B and R-32 behave at the gauges. Then the installation side: charge limits and room size, leak detection sensors, control boards and mitigation. Finally the service side: ignition sources, ventilation, tools, brazing practice and what to do when a mitigation alarm trips.

Everything here is general education. Charge limits, sensor requirements and procedures come from the equipment manufacturer, the applicable safety standards and your local code, and they differ by product. Always follow the installation manual for the unit in front of you, and treat training from the manufacturer or your employer as the authority on tools and procedure.
