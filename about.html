About

Trade Study Bench is a small independent project by a working HVAC and boiler technician in New Jersey.

The aim is simple: explain the parts of the trade that textbooks make hard, in plain English, with sources you can check. The author works in residential, commercial and industrial settings, including steam systems and chilled-water plants.

About the author

Trade Study Bench is run by Max Winkler, a working HVAC and boiler technician in New Jersey. Training: an associate's degree in HVAC (Pennsylvania College of Technology, 2021). Experience: residential service, commercial equipment and industrial plant work, including steam pressure-reducing stations, chillers and heat exchangers. The author also runs a small New Jersey HVAC and handyman business, Winkler Mechanical, which is separate from this site.

How a lesson is checked: facts come from public sources that are linked at the bottom of every lesson, such as EPA and CDC pages, manufacturer installation sheets and the codes and rules themselves. Numbers that are only typical examples say so. If you find a mistake, please tell us through the contact page and we will fix it and update the date on the page.

How the material is made

  • Written in original wording from public sources, guided by field experience.
  • No real exam questions and no copied code-book or manual text.
  • Each lesson lists its sources. Where a number is only a typical example, it says so.
  • Lessons and study packs are researched and drafted with the help of AI writing tools, working only from the sources listed on each page, and then checked against those sources. Max is personally reviewing the material, and paid products are reviewed by him before they go on sale. Corrections are welcome through the contact page.

Content on this site is AI-drafted from the cited sources and reviewed by Max Winkler.

Who it is for

Students, apprentices and working techs. Lessons are written to be readable by anyone, including younger students exploring the trades.
